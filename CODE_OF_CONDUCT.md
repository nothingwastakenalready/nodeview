# code of conduct

be decent.

this is a small project. that does not make it a place for drive-by cruelty,
harassment, threats, spam, bigotry or weird power games.

## expected

- be clear
- be patient with people who are trying
- disagree on the work, not on the person
- assume the project is allowed to be unfinished
- keep security reports private until there is a fix or a safe disclosure path

## not welcome

- harassment or targeted insults
- sexualized comments or unwanted attention
- doxxing or sharing private information
- pushing people to expose credentials, devices or private network details
- using issues as a support desk for unrelated problems

## enforcement

maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove comments and may block accounts.

that is not a court process. it is just keeping the room usable.
